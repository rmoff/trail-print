    How to print it
      • Layers of 0.12 to 0.16 mm make the slopes smooth.
      • No supports needed. Print it flat, text side down.
      • Colour changes need a purge tower beside the tile: the see-through block on the plate in the preview. If the slicer puts it on top of the tile, drag the tower into the free corner.
      • The 3MF opens in Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer with each part already on its filament. For PrusaSlicer or Cura, use the other 3MF and assign the parts to filaments yourself.
